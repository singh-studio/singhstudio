Questions

How do I enter a fours team?

Type the team into one cell with commas between the names, and tick “Team” on that column. The board shows the team as you wrote it, and each person gets the honour.

Can we show who skipped?

Write it however you normally do, for example “J. Smith (skip)”. Notes in brackets show on the board but are ignored when matching people.

Our clubhouse TV is old. Will it work?

It needs something that runs a web browser plugged into it, like an old laptop. The TV itself only needs an HDMI socket.
