The boards it sets up for you

Start from the golf club template and these boards are ready for your names. Rename them, add your own, or remove any you don’t keep.

  • Club Champions (Men): year, champion
  • Club Champions (Women): year, champion
  • Club Champions (Juniors): year, champion
  • Course Records: tees, holder, score, year
  • Presidents: years, president
  • Club Captains: year, men’s captain, women’s captain
  • Life Members: elected, life member
  • Hole in One: year, name, hole

Add your own as well: pennants, matchplay, foursomes, junior pennants or any trophy with a history.

Questions

Can it show competition results or leaderboards?

Laurel is for honours, the names that stay for good. Live scores and leaderboards belong in your golf software; Laurel can sit on the same TV between them.

We already have beautiful timber boards.

Keep them. Leave the history on the wall up to the year it filled, and put everything since, plus the whole archive, on the screen and website.

How do we get eighty years of names in?

Copy them from a spreadsheet if you have one, type them, or send me photos of the boards and I’ll do it for you.
