InductedNameField
1972Dr Anahera TipeneMedicine

Dux in 1972, and later the doctor who set up the free clinic on Harbour Road. Still volunteers there on Thursdays.

1981Kevin FaleoloSport

Played 52 tests for Samoa and came home to coach the First XV for a decade.

Marine scientist whose work on the harbour’s seagrass beds changed how the region manages its estuaries.

2003Rawiri MoanaArts

Composer and kapa haka tutor; wrote the school waiata sung at every prizegiving since 2006.
