The boards it sets up for you

Start from the sports club template and these boards are ready for your names. Rename them, add your own, or remove any you don’t keep.

  • Premiership Winners: year, grade, captain
  • Player of the Year: year, player
  • Club Captains: year, captain
  • Presidents: years, president
  • Life Members: elected, life member
  • Centurions (100 games): year, player, games

Add your own as well: coaches, players of the year by grade, or representative honours from region to national team.

Questions

Can we have a board for each grade?

Yes, or one board with a Grade column. One board per honour, with the grade beside the year, usually reads best.

Does it work for cricket, netball, hockey, football, rowing and surf?

Yes. Boards are just lists you set up, with your own column headings, so they fit any sport.

Can it show sponsors?

Laurel is for honours. Your sponsors can share the same TV through your normal screen setup.
