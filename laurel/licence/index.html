Licence, terms and privacy.

Laurel is sold by Singh Studio, Aotearoa New Zealand. Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record: they process your payment, charge any GST, VAT or sales tax that applies where you are, and email your receipt and licence key.

What you can do

Club licence: use Laurel to make honours boards for one club, school or organisation, and show them on any number of screens, websites and touchscreens that organisation runs, for as long as you like.

Trade licence: use Laurel to make honours boards for any number of organisations, including as part of a paid service to your clients, and remove the small Laurel mark from boards you make. Your clients may keep using the boards after your work for them ends.

With either licence you may change the board files you make for the organisations the licence covers.

What you can’t do

You may not sell, share or publish the Laurel editor, or offer Laurel itself as a product or service for others to make their own boards. Putting the boards you make on screens and websites is exactly what Laurel is for; giving other people the tool is not.

The free trial

You can use every part of Laurel free. Files made without a licence show a small “Laurel trial” ribbon. You’re welcome to use trial files, ribbon and all.

Licence keys

Your key is in your receipt email. Enter it in the editor once while online; the editor checks it with Lemon Squeezy and then works offline. Each key can be active on a few computers at once, so a committee can share the work. Moving computers? Deactivate the key on the old one from the editor.

Updates and help

Updates to version 1 are free for both licences. Help is by email, offered in good faith, usually within two working days.

Refunds

Because everything can be tried free before you buy, purchases aren’t refundable. If Laurel isn’t working the way you hoped, email kris@singhstudio.co.nz and I’ll help you get it working.

No warranty

Laurel is provided as it is. Singh Studio isn’t liable for any loss arising from its use, to the extent the law allows. You’re responsible for the names and words you publish with it, including having permission to publish them where that’s needed.

Privacy

The editor runs in your browser. Your boards, names and pictures are saved in that browser on your computer and in the files you download. They are never sent to Singh Studio or anyone else.

Licence checks send your licence key, and a label made from your organisation’s name and type of computer, to Lemon Squeezy, so they can confirm the key. This happens when you activate, about once a week after that while you use the editor online, and when you deactivate.

Board files contain no tracking, cookies or analytics, and don’t contact anyone. A board hosted on a website checks that same website for a newer copy of itself every ten minutes, so screens update after you upload a new file.

These pages may use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and clicks on buy buttons; they don’t use advertising cookies. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy covers your payment details.
