The boards it sets up for you

Start from the school template and these boards are ready for your names. Rename them, add your own, or remove any you don’t keep.

  • Dux: year, dux
  • Proxime Accessit: year, name
  • Head Students: year, head boy, head girl
  • Sportsperson of the Year: year, name, sport
  • Hall of Fame (Old students): inducted, name, field

Add your own as well: cultural and service awards, scholarships, prefects, house captains or principals.

Why it suits schools

Current students stay offline

Any board can be kept off the website with one switch, and its names are left out of the website file completely, not just hidden.

Prizegiving, reunions and open days

Run the slideshow on the hall projector at prizegiving, and send old students the website link before a reunion.

Works with the screen you have

Run it on its own screen, or add its web address as a slide in the signage software your school already uses.

Honours with a story

Hall of Fame inductees get a photo and a citation on their own slide, so students see what their predecessors went on to do.

Questions

Do we need consent to publish names?

Laurel shows only what you enter. Inside the school, a screen is no different from the boards you already have. For the website, follow your school’s privacy policy, and keep any board off the internet with one switch.

Can it show te reo Māori?

Yes. Macrons display properly in every style, and you can rename any word on the boards, such as “Honours” or the search prompt.

Who looks after it when staff change?

Keep the backup file in the school’s shared drive. Anyone can open it in the editor next year and carry on.
