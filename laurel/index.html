Why go digital

Timber boards are beautiful. They’re also full.

Laurel keeps the look and the dignity of a real honours board, and takes away the three things every club secretary knows too well.

The wall runs out

Sooner or later the last board has no room left, and another one means another fundraiser and another stretch of wall you don’t have.

Every name costs

New names wait for the signwriter and arrive with a bill, every season. A spelling mistake is there for good.

Nobody can find themselves

Eighty years of champions across a dozen boards, and a grandchild visiting can’t find Grandad. The names deserve better than a squint from across the room.

How it works

Three steps. One afternoon.

The editor runs in your browser and saves as you go. There’s nothing to install and no account to make, and your names never leave your computer while you work.

  1. Type or paste your names

    Copy straight from Excel, Google Sheets or a Word document. Laurel splits “1987 J. Smith” into the year and the name for you, and lines up columns from a spreadsheet.

  2. Choose a look

    Timber & gold, Heritage or Modern, with your crest and your club colours. A live preview shows it on a TV, a phone and a touchscreen as you go.

  3. Save one file

    Download it for the TV, the website or a touchscreen. It works offline, runs on almost anything with a browser, and it’s yours to keep.

One file, three places

On the TV. On the website. In their hands.

The same names and the same look everywhere, so the honours in the clubhouse and the honours online always agree.

01 · The clubhouse TV

A slideshow with the dignity of the real thing.

A cover with your crest, this year’s honours, then every board in turn. Long lists flow across pages by themselves, in landscape or portrait.

  • Runs from a laptop, a mini PC or a streaming stick
  • A QR code lets members look themselves up on their phones
  • Drifts a hair between slides to protect screens from burn-in
  • Re-upload the file and screens loading it from your website refresh themselves

02 · Your website

Every member gets a page of their own.

A searchable honours page people can use from home and share. Each person’s page gathers every honour they’ve earned across every board.

  • Search ignores accents, so “henare” finds Hēnare
  • Type a year to see everyone honoured that year
  • Readable by search engines, screen readers and printers
  • No tracking, no cookies, no accounts

03 · A touchscreen in the foyer

Plays until someone touches it.

The slideshow runs until someone walks up, then it turns into a search with big surname buttons. When they walk away it goes back to the slideshow on its own.

  • Big A–Z buttons for browsing by surname
  • Works on any touchscreen that runs a browser
  • Back to the slideshow after 90 seconds

Questions

Before you start.

Something not covered here? Email kris@singhstudio.co.nz and you’ll hear back from me, the person who made it.

What do we need for the TV?

Any TV with an HDMI socket, plus something that runs a web browser: an old laptop, a small mini PC, or a streaming stick with a browser. Open the file, press F for full screen and leave it running. The setup guide walks through each option.

Do we need the internet?

No. Everything is inside the file: names, fonts, crest and pictures, so it runs offline. You only need the internet to put the board on your website, or if you want a streaming stick to load it from there.

How do members search from home?

Download the website file and put it on your club website, or on a free host like Netlify. Then add that address in Laurel, and your TV shows a QR code to it. Here’s how.

How do we add names next season?

Open the editor, open your file (or carry on where you left off), add the new names and download again. Replace the old file on the TV or website. A screen that loads the board from your website notices the new file and refreshes itself.

How many names will it hold?

Thousands. Long boards flow across as many pages as they need on the TV, and the website lists everything with search and a filter on each board.

We have a portrait TV in the hallway.

Good: Laurel lays boards out to suit a tall screen, the way a real honours board hangs.

Can we see it with our own names before we pay?

Yes, and I’d encourage it. The free trial does everything with your names, on your own TV. Files made in the trial carry a small “Laurel trial” ribbon until you buy a licence.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, and every update to version 1 is included.

What if Singh Studio disappears?

Your files keep working. They don’t call home, need an account or rely on my servers. You can download the editor itself and keep it, too.

Do you collect any data?

No. While you work, your names stay in your own browser. Checking a licence key sends the key to Lemon Squeezy, my payment provider, and nothing else. The boards themselves have no tracking or cookies. Privacy details.

Can we keep some names off the internet?

Yes. Any board can be kept off the website and touchscreen with one switch, and its names are left out of those files completely. Schools use it for current students.

Refunds?

Because you can try everything free first, purchases aren’t refundable. If it isn’t working the way you hoped, email me and I’ll help you get it right.
