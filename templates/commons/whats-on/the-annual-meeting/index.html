Last year the house cost $148,600 to run. This is where it came from and where it went.

Grants$52,000
Gifts$41,200

Koha and fees$17,000

Money out
Wages$78,200
Food$35,400

Who it is for

Anyone who uses the house, volunteers here, lives nearby or would like to hear where the money goes.

Judith Lowe chairs the board of five, and the 64 volunteers on the roster are all welcome to ask what they like.

Getting in

  • Step-free from Tide Street
  • An accessible toilet
  • A hearing loop in the hall
  • A quiet room if the noise gets to be too much
  • Four car parks and two mobility parks at the door
