Bike fix day

27SaturdayFebruary10am to 1pm

Bring the bike that has been in the shed for a year. Hemi and two others will be in the car park with twelve stands, and parts are sold at cost.

To save a place, email hello@tidestreet.example or call 04 555 0129.

Hemi has the tools laid out from 10am, with two others to help.

Parts are sold at cost, so a tube or a cable costs what it cost us. Nothing is charged for the stand or the help.

What happens, in order

  1. 1

    10am

    The stands go up in the car park, and Hemi has the tools laid out on a table.

  2. 2

    Your turn

    Your bike goes on its stand. Someone looks it over with you and says what they think is wrong.

  3. 3

    Fix it together

    You do the work with someone beside you, so you know what to do next time. Parts come at cost.

  4. 4

    1pm

    The stands come down, and you ride home.

What to bring

The bike, and anything you already know it needs.

Anyone with a bike can come, whatever age it is and whatever shape it is in. You do not have to know how to fix it, because that is what the morning is for.

Bring

  • The bike
  • Any part you already have for it
  • A drink bottle

We have

  • Twelve stands and the tools
  • Hemi and two others
  • Parts at cost
  • A kettle in the hall

Getting in

  • Step-free from Tide Street
  • An accessible toilet
  • A hearing loop in the hall
  • A quiet room if the noise gets to be too much
  • Four car parks and two mobility parks at the door
