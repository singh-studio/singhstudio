The long lunch

13SundayDecemberNoon to 2.30pm

One very long table in the main hall, set for ninety, on the second Sunday of December. It is free, and Graeme is doing the hams.

What happens, in order

  1. 1

    Noon

    The doors open, and the long table is already laid. Saved places are marked, and anyone without one takes a free plate.

  2. 2

    Then lunch

    The hams come to the table, carved by Graeme, alongside every salad that came through the door.

  3. 3

    Puddings

    Whatever came in a dish gets passed down the table, and the urn is on the bench for tea.

  4. 4

    2.30pm

    Lunch is over, and everyone who can helps put the chairs and trestles away.

Who it is for

Anyone. Neighbours, families, people on their own and people who have never been inside the hall.

Children sit with their grown-ups, and there are seats near the doors and next to the kitchen for anyone who would rather not be in the middle of the table.

Getting in

  • Step-free from Tide Street
  • An accessible toilet
  • A hearing loop in the hall
  • A quiet room if the noise gets to be too much
  • Four car parks and two mobility parks at the door
