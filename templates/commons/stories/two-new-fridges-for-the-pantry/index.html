Two new fridges for the pantry

Friday 13 November, 12 days ago 1 minute read

Two new fridges came to the pantry on a trailer, and by the following Thursday both of them were full.

Until then the eggs and the other cold things had to share space in the kitchen fridge, which Graeme Pollock also needs for Kai Night on Tuesdays. The new fridges stand along the wall near the pass to the hall, and Hemi Walker, who looks after the building, found them a power point of their own.

Last Thursday’s table shows what they are for. It held 40 bags of potatoes, 36 loaves, 24 dozen eggs, 52 tins of tomatoes and 18 boxes of nappies. The eggs are the reason for the fridges, and the drawing above shows them on the shelves.

About 40 households come through the pantry each week. Each of them chooses what goes in their own bag, nobody is asked to explain, and nobody has to sign up first. It runs on Thursdays from 10am to noon.

The pantry is still short of cooking oil, tinned fish and nappies in size 4. Any of them can be left at the hall on a Thursday morning, and bags are always welcome too. There are spares at the door for anyone who forgets theirs, and a trolley to borrow.

A pantry packer arrives at 9 on Thursday mornings to set the table out before the doors open at 10. The house needs one more person for that hour, and the Help out page has the details.
