The plum tree needs picking

Saturday 21 November, 4 days ago 1 minute read

The plum tree at the back of the garden is a January tree, and the house is closed from 24 December to 20 January. That leaves a gap, and the people who run Garden morning would like to fill it with buckets.

Garden morning is on every other Saturday from 9 to 11am, with gloves and tea supplied. The first one after the summer break is the morning to aim for, because the plums will be at their ripest and the tree will not wait.

The tree is tall, and the best fruit is well above head height. A ladder and two long-handled pickers live in the shed beside the raised beds. Someone standing at the bottom to hold the ladder and catch what falls is every bit as useful as someone at the top.

Anyone can pick. Bring a bucket, an ice-cream container or a bag, because whatever you pick can go home with you. Plums that are too soft to travel are best eaten on the spot with a cup of the tea.

January is also when the beans come in, so there is picking for people who would rather keep both feet on the ground. The garden has six beds, and there is something to do at every one of them.

Boots or shoes that can get muddy are the one thing worth bringing besides the bucket, and a hat if the morning is bright. The paths to the beds are step-free, with seats along the way for anyone who wants to pick sitting down.

The next Garden morning is on Saturday 28 November, and it is a good one to come to if you would like to find out where the ladder is kept before the plums arrive.
