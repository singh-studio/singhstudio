Getting here

The route on the map is the one chosen above. Choose another way and the steps change with it.

There are five ways here, each written out in order.

By train

  1. Take the train to the station and come out on the Tide Street side.
  2. Cross Station Road and walk straight up Tide Street. It takes about five minutes.
  3. The house is on your right, the old timber hall set back from the footpath.
  4. Follow the front path from the footpath to the door. It is flat all the way.

By bus

  1. Get off at the stop on Marine Road, beside the harbour.
  2. Walk east along Marine Road to Tide Street and turn right.
  3. Walk down Tide Street with the harbour behind you. The house is on your left.
  4. Follow the front path from the footpath to the door. It is flat all the way.

By bike

  1. Ride east along Station Road, the street that runs beside the railway.
  2. Turn left into Tide Street. The house is on your right.
  3. Lean the bike on the fence by the front path.
  4. Follow the front path to the door. It is flat all the way.

By car

  1. Turn into Station Road from Salt Lane and drive west.
  2. There are four car parks and two mobility parks on the right, along the front of the house.
  3. The two mobility parks are the ones nearest the path.
  4. Follow the path from the car park to the door. It is flat all the way.

On foot

  1. Walk west along Marine Road, with the harbour on your right.
  2. Turn left into Tide Street. The park is on your right.
  3. Walk down Tide Street. The house is on your left.
  4. Follow the front path from the footpath to the door. It is flat all the way.

From the footpath to a seat

  1. The footpath along Tide Street is flat, and the front path runs from it to the door without a step.
  2. Someone on the door will say hello and point you at the tea, which is always on the bench.
  3. The hall is straight ahead and has a hearing loop. Ask at the door if you would like it switched on.
  4. If it gets loud or you need a breather, the quiet room is off the hall and free to anyone.
  5. The accessible toilet is on the same level as the hall, and whoever is on the door will show you where.
  6. Then you pick a seat. At Kai Night there are ninety, and any free one is yours.

There are four car parks and two mobility parks at the door.
