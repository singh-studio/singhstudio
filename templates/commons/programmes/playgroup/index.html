A Friday morning, in order

Five things happen between half past nine and half past eleven, and they happen in the same order every week.

  1. 9.30

    Blocks

    The blocks come out onto the rug, and the babies have a warm corner of their own.

  2. 10.00

    Paint

    The messy corner opens. A change of clothes is a good idea.

  3. 10.30

    Fruit and coffee

    Orange segments for the little ones and hot coffee for the grown-ups.

  4. 11.00

    Songs

    Everyone sits on the rug and sings, and nobody has to know the words.

  5. 11.20

    Pack-up

    The blocks go back in the basket and the coats go back on.

What’s in the room

  • Wooden blocks on a big rug
  • A warm corner for babies
  • A messy corner for paint
  • Fruit and hot coffee on the bench
  • A baby change table, and a kettle that is always on
