Te reo for beginners

Eight Wednesday evenings in a ring of eighteen chairs, starting from hello. It is $40 a term, and a few places are always kept for koha.

What to expect

You sit in the ring with a notebook and a pen, and most of the evening is talking. Tea is on the bench, so help yourself when you arrive.

Week one starts at hello, so you do not need to know a single word first. By the eighth week you will be able to stand up and say who you are and where you are from.

Enrol for next term

Give us your name and pick a place. We will keep a chair for you from Wednesday 3 February. If you choose a koha place, that is all we ask.
