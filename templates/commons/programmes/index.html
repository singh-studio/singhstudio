Not sure where to start

Come to a Kai Night. There is a seat and a plate for anyone who turns up on a Tuesday, and you will meet most of the house over dinner. By the time the dishes start you will know which of the others sounds like you.

The summer break

The house is shut from 24 December to 20 January, which is how 52 weeks become 48. Everything starts again in the week of 21 January.
