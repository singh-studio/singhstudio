Homework Club

A quiet room, snacks on arrival and a tutor at every table, twice a week after school. It is free, and it is for years 4 to 10.

Enrol a childSee a session

The facts

When
Mondays and Wednesdays, 3.30 to 5pmSnacks are out at 3.30pm.
Cost
FreeThere is nothing to pay for the tutoring or the snacks.
Where
The main hallThe quiet room is free if a kid would rather work alone.
Who runs it
Priya NairPriya leads, with 11 tutors who are all police vetted.
Who it is for
Years 4 to 1032 kids are on the roster.
Bring
HomeworkAnd a water bottle and your school bag.

The room from above

Eight tables with four kids at each, and eleven tutors on the larger plates. Eight sit beside a table and three move around the room.

What a session looks like

A tutor stays with each table, so everyone at it has someone close by when they get stuck.

  1. 13.30

    Snacks

    Snacks are out while everyone arrives, and nobody has to start yet.

    15 minutes

  2. 23.45

    Sort out what’s due

    Each kid says what is due and when, and the tutor helps put it in order.

    15 minutes

  3. 34.00

    Work

    Fifty minutes at the table. The tutors move around, and the quiet room is free.

    50 minutes

  4. 44.50

    Pack up

    Bags packed and tables wiped, with ten minutes left before 5pm.

    10 minutes

Enrol a child

Tell us your name and your child’s year, and Priya will keep a place at the next session. Tell us what they are stuck on if you like, and the tutor will have it in mind.

To enrol a child, email hello@tidestreet.example or call 04 555 0129.

If you would like to be a tutor, Help out has the details, and tutors are police vetted first.
