Val came for a hem and stayed

Val brought her curtains to the Repair Café in February and asked someone to take them up. By May she was hemming other people’s. Now she comes on the first Saturday of every month with her own sewing basket and has a small waiting list.

Who you’ll meet

Six neighbours took on the lease in March 2012, when the hall had a leaking roof and a very good stove. The first Kai Night fed eleven people. The roof is fixed now and the stove is still going.

  • Moana Reweti runs the house Here most days
  • Graeme Pollock cooks Tuesdays
  • Priya Nair leads homework club Mondays and Wednesdays
  • Hemi Walker mends things and minds the building First Saturdays
  • Ana Fifita looks after volunteers Thursdays
  • Judith Lowe chairs the board Every other Saturday, in the garden
