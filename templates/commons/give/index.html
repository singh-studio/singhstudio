Every week

A plate laid every Tuesday is $6 a week. We are open for 48 weeks of the year, so one plate a week comes to $288 across the year.

  • 1 plate a week is $6, and $288 over the year
  • 5 plates a week is $30, and $1,440 over the year
  • 90 plates a week is $540, and $25,920 over the year
Try your own number
