What is free

December 2026

Each day has three half circles, for the morning, the afternoon and the evening. An empty one is free and a filled one is taken by something we run. Press a day to see its three slots. A whole plate marks the long lunch, which fills its Sunday.

Sundays go to the groups that already hire the hall. The second Sunday of December is the long lunch, and the hall is not hired out that day. In the week the hall is taken on Monday and Wednesday afternoons, Tuesday and Wednesday evenings, and Thursday and Friday mornings, and on the first Saturday of the month the Repair Café has it from ten until one. The house is closed from 24 December for the summer break. Email hello@tidestreet.example to ask about any other time.

House rules

  1. Leave it as you found it

    Chairs stacked, trestles folded and the floor swept before you go.

  2. Finish by the time you booked

    The next group is usually waiting at the door.

  3. No smoking or vaping

    That covers the hall, the front steps and the car park.

  4. Tell us if something breaks

    Hemi would sooner hear it from you on the day.
