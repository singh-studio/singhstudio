What we do not do

We do not sell what you give us, give it to another organisation or use it for anything except what is in the list above. The house does not ask for card numbers or bank details on this site.

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. Your choices in Try it on and any seats you save are kept in your browser for as long as the tab is open. They are not sent anywhere, and they are gone when you close the tab.

Seeing what we hold

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see what we hold about you and to have it corrected. Email hello@tidestreet.example or call 04 555 0129 and Moana Reweti will send you everything we have. If you ask us to delete it we will, apart from receipts that we have to keep.

If you are not happy with how we handled something, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz. We would rather hear it first, so please write to us as well.
