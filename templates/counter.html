Our story

A café built around the neighbourhood.

We opened with four tables, a secondhand Linea and a theory: if the coffee's right and the welcome's real, Petone would do the rest. It did.

The fish comes off the wharf, the veges from the Saturday market, and half the art on the walls is by regulars under ten.
