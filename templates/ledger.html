What we do

Four disciplines, one ledger

Everything here is priced up front and delivered on a schedule you can set your watch to.

01

Compliance & tax

Annual accounts, income tax, GST and FBT returns filed early, not at the deadline. We flag issues in June, not the following April.

02

Advisory & forecasting

Cashflow forecasts, budget-to-actual reviews, and quarterly sit-downs that turn your numbers into decisions you can act on.

03

Payroll & bookkeeping

Weekly or fortnightly pay runs, PAYE handled correctly the first time, and monthly books reconciled before you ask for them.

04

Xero setup & training

Chart of accounts built for your industry, bank feeds connected properly, and a training session so your team isn't guessing.
