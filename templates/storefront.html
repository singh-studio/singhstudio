Poured in Petone.

North End started as one saucepan on a flat stove and a nose that wouldn’t quit. Six years on we pour every candle by hand in a workshop above the old butcher’s on Jackson Street.

Soy wax, cotton wicks, fragrance blended in-house. Nothing in the jar we couldn’t pronounce at the market stall where it all began.
