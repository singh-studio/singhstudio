Privacy

Privacy

This page covers the plugin store and the plugins themselves.

The plugins

The plugins collect nothing. They read your own website’s public content and keep a copy, and things like a listener’s place in a sermon, in each visitor’s own browser. Nothing is sent to Singh Studio or anyone else.

Buying a plugin

Payments are handled by our payment provider as merchant of record. They hold your payment and billing details under their own privacy policy, and share your name, email address and order with us so we can deliver the plugin, send updates and help you.

This website

If analytics is switched on, we use Google Analytics with IP anonymisation to see which pages are useful. You can block it with any common browser setting or extension and the site still works.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you later. Ask and we’ll delete it.

Contact

Singh Studio, Aotearoa New Zealand. kris@singhstudio.co.nz. Your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 apply.
