Harbourlight Church · Hall roof appeal

A new roof for the hall

The hall roof has kept the rain off playgroup and Friday youth since 1974, and this winter it finally gave up. We’re raising $180,000 to replace it before the next one, and every gift goes straight to the roof.

Where we’re up to

This bar reads its numbers from a small CSV file, the same shape a published Google Sheet gives you, so the treasurer can update the total without touching the website. The page itself still says $109,000, and the sheet wins.

data-style="thermometer"

Or as a thermometer

The same numbers drawn the old-fashioned way, which suits a narrow column beside your own words. The milestones sit on the glass and fill in as the total passes them.

Thanks to everyone who’s given so far. We’ll update this every Monday.
