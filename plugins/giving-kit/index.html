Install

One paste and about ten minutes.

The download includes a step-by-step guide written for whoever looks after your website. Read it before you buy.

Add a Code block where you want the appeal and paste in the one file from the download.

Change the goal, the amount raised, the currency and your giving link on the first line, and add milestones or gift levels if you want them.

Open the live page with ?give=check on the end to see what Giving Kit found and anything worth fixing.

What you need: A Squarespace plan that runs JavaScript (Core, Plus or Advanced, or Business and Commerce on older plans), on version 7.0 or 7.1, and a page where people can give, such as a Squarespace donation page or Givealittle.

Questions

The questions people ask first.

Does Giving Kit take the money?

No. It sends people to the giving page you already use, such as a Squarespace donation page, Givealittle, PayPal or Stripe, with the amount and frequency in the link when that page can read them. The money side stays exactly where it is now.

Which tax rules does it know?

New Zealand’s donation tax credit (a third back on gifts of $5 or more), UK Gift Aid (25% more to the charity) and Australian deductions (with an estimate at the donor’s tax rate, labelled as one). It shows none unless you turn one on, and the small print under the calculator says what the figures depend on.

Can the total update by itself?

Yes, from a Google Sheet published to the web as CSV, with rows for raised, goal and donors. Google can take a few minutes to refresh a published sheet, and if it can’t be read Giving Kit uses the numbers on the page, so the appeal never shows an error.

Can I show just a thermometer?

Yes. Each block can be the full appeal, the progress bar or thermometer, the calculator or the gift levels, and you can have several on one page, such as a thermometer at the top and a calculator further down.

What happens when we pass our goal?

The bar stays full, a Goal reached badge appears and the percentage keeps counting, so it might say 112%. The setup check mentions it in case you’d like to raise the goal.

Can I link straight to a suggested pledge?

Yes. Adding ?give=50-weekly-3y to the page address opens the calculator at $50 a week for three years, which is handy in a newsletter or an email to regular givers.
