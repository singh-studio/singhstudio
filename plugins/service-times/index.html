Install

One paste and about ten minutes.

The download includes a step-by-step guide written for whoever looks after your website. Read it before you buy.

Add a Code block where you want your service times and paste in the one file from the download.

Type your services, specials and livestream link into the settings at the top of the paste.

Open the live page with ?services=check on the end to see your next six services and anything worth fixing.

What you need: A Squarespace plan that runs JavaScript (Core, Plus or Advanced, or Business and Commerce on older plans), on version 7.0 or 7.1.

Questions

The questions people ask first.

Does it connect to our calendar or our YouTube channel?

No, and it doesn’t need to. You type your services once and it works out every week from them. The live banner is timed from your schedule and links to your stream’s address, so it can’t tell whether the stream itself has started.

How do I handle Christmas and Easter?

Add them as specials with a date and time. On that day the usual services are replaced, or only the ones you name. A skip takes a single service off for a week, with a note such as Summer break.

Do I need to change anything when the clocks change?

No. Services are worked out in your church’s time zone, so the 9:30 service stays at 9:30 through the April and September changes.

Can people add a service to their calendar?

Yes. The card offers an .ics file for Apple Calendar and Outlook, or a Google Calendar link, for that one service with its time, place and stream link.

Can it show on every page?

Yes. Put the settings once in Code Injection and add a short line wherever you want the bar, the card, the schedule or the live banner.

What if I make a mistake in the settings?

Curly quotes, comments and stray commas are tidied so the page still works. If something can’t be read, visitors just see “Service times are being updated” while the setup check lists the problem in plain English, down to the line.
