Install

One paste and about ten minutes.

The download includes a step-by-step guide written for whoever looks after your website. Read it before you buy.

Add a Code block to the page where you want the library and paste in the one file from the download.

Change /sermons to your sermons blog’s address. That’s the only setting you have to touch.

Save and open the page, then add ?shelf=check to the address to see what it found.

What you need: A Squarespace plan that runs JavaScript (Core, Plus or Advanced, or the older Business and Commerce plans) and your sermons in a public blog.

Questions

The questions people ask first.

Do we have to change how we post sermons?

No. It reads the blog posts you already have. A category for each series, the passage in the title or a “Scripture:” tag, and the speaker’s name in the audio block make it richer, and the setup check tells you exactly what’s missing.

Our sermons are on YouTube. Does that work?

If each sermon has a blog post with the YouTube or Vimeo video in it, the shelf shows a Watch link for it. The built-in player plays audio from a Squarespace audio block or a linked MP3.

Will it slow our site down?

It only runs on the page you add it to. The code is about 25 KB compressed, it loads your own blog from your own site, and after the first visit the archive is remembered in the visitor’s browser, so a return visit takes one quick request.

Does it collect any data?

No. There’s no tracking and no outside server. Listening positions and the cached archive stay in each visitor’s own browser.

Can we use our own wording or another language?

Yes. Every label can be changed with a few lines, and you can add other names for the books of the Bible, for example in te reo Māori.

Will it work on Squarespace 7.0?

Yes. I tested it on a live 7.0 church archive with more than 700 sermons and on 7.1 sites, in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.
