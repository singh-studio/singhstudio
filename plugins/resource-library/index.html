Questions

The questions people ask first.

Do I have to rebuild my resources page?

Probably not. If your resources are already blog posts, the library reads what’s there: titles, thumbnails, excerpts, categories, tags and the links in each post. The setup check shows what it found and what would make it better.

Where should the files live?

Anywhere your posts can link to. Files uploaded to Squarespace download straight from the card, and Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive and SharePoint links open in a new tab. YouTube and Vimeo videos get a Watch button.

What if a post has lots of files?

A card with two to four files shows “3 files” and opens a list of them. With five or more, the main button opens the list, since downloading whichever file comes first wouldn’t help anyone.

How many resources can it handle?

I’ve tested it on a live news library of 145 posts. The first page of cards shows within about a second, the whole library had loaded in under five seconds on a first visit, and returning visitors get it in a fraction of a second, because the library keeps a copy in their browser and checks it with one request.

Can I change the words?

Yes. Every label can be changed with a few lines above the paste, which also covers other languages. The install guide lists them all.

Does it work on the Basic or Personal plan?

No. Those plans don’t run custom scripts. You need Core, Plus or Advanced, or one of the older Business or Commerce plans.
