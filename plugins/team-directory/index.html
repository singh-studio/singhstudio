Install

One paste and about ten minutes.

The download includes a step-by-step guide written for whoever looks after your website. Read it before you buy.

Make a blog for your people (it can stay out of your menus) and add one post per person.

Add a Code block to your team page and paste in the file from the download.

Change /team-members to your blog’s address, save, and look at the live page.

What you need: A Squarespace 7.0 or 7.1 site on a plan that runs JavaScript (Core, Plus or Advanced, or Business and Commerce on older plans), and a public blog to hold your people. The blog can be hidden from your navigation and from search engines.

Questions

The questions people ask first.

Do I have to rebuild my team page?

No. The directory goes in a Code block on whichever page you like, and the people live in a separate blog that can stay out of your menus. If you already keep staff bios as blog posts, it can read those.

Will it work on my plan?

If your plan runs JavaScript, yes. That’s Core, Plus and Advanced on the current plans, and Business, Commerce Basic and Commerce Advanced on older ones. The Basic and Personal plans block custom scripts. It works on Squarespace 7.0 and 7.1.

What if some people don’t have a photo or a role yet?

They still show. People without a photo get a monogram of their initials, and the role line is simply left out. The setup check names who’s missing what, so you can fill the gaps when you have time.

Can a page show just one team?

Yes. data-start="Board" opens the directory on the Board, and visitors can still press Everyone. You can also link straight to a team or a person from a newsletter, for example ?team=t/youth-families.

Does it look like my site?

It takes your fonts, colours and button shape from the section it sits in, light or dark, so it looks like part of the page. Every label can be changed too, including into te reo Māori.

Is anyone’s information sent anywhere?

No. It reads your own public blog and keeps a copy in each visitor’s browser. There’s no outside server and no tracking, and it only shows what you’ve already published in those posts.
