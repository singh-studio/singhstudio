Amplify

A conference website I rebuilt three years running, with a new look each time.

Book a call
Client
The Salvation Army NZ, Youth
Years
2023 to 2025
My part
  • Design & front-end build
  • Registration flow
  • Motion on the site
  • A CMS the team updates

At a glance

What they needed

The Salvation Army’s national youth conference needed a website that could change its look every year and let people register easily, and it had to feel like the event itself, which is loud and young.

What I did

I designed and built the website each year and set up the registration.

What changed

The youth team updates the site themselves.

A new look every year

Each conference came with its own theme, and I carried its type, colour and motion across the site.

3 consecutive builds: 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Built for the team to run

I kept the registration and the page structure plain on purpose, because parents and youth leaders use it too.

I set up the content so the team can update programmes and speakers without touching code.

The result

The best result I can point to is that the client asked me back, twice.
