/ Setup & support

Pair once.
Then tap to connect.

Home Mac is in private testing. The public Mac companion download is not available yet.

Set up your Mac

  1. Install the free Home Mac Companion from the Home Mac product page when available. Move it to Applications and open it.
  2. Allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when prompted. These let the companion show your desktop and carry out your clicks and keystrokes. Quit and reopen if macOS asks.
  3. Keep the Mac awake and unlocked on your home network. Click Pair an iPhone.

Connect your iPhone or iPad

  1. Join the same network, open Home Mac, and allow Local Network access.
  2. Tap Pair a Mac. Allow Camera access to scan the temporary code on your Mac. You can also paste a pairing code.
  3. Next time, tap your saved Mac. Treat pairing codes as private; they expire after five minutes.

Control the desktop

Tap to click, double-tap to double-click, or hold and drag. Two-finger tap is right-click. Pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers. Tap the floating sliders button for the keyboard, scroll arrows and view options. Disconnect is inside view options.

Fill screen crops the edges; Show whole desktop can leave space because your screens have different shapes. Choose the option that suits your task.

If you can’t connect

  • Check both devices are on the same local network. Guest networks and network isolation can block device-to-device connections.
  • Wake and unlock the Mac. Reopen the companion and choose Start sharing.
  • In iPhone Settings, check Home Mac has Local Network access.
  • Check Screen Recording and Accessibility for Home Mac in Mac System Settings. Use Check again in the companion; if needed, quit and reopen it.
  • If pairing has been revoked, remove the saved Mac on your phone and pair again with a fresh code.

Stop access or remove a device

Choose Pause in the Mac companion to stop sharing. Remove a paired device there to revoke its credential. Removing a Mac from your iPhone deletes its saved connection and key; revoke on the Mac too if a device is lost.

Contact support

Email kris@singhstudio.co.nz with your iPhone model, iOS/macOS versions, Home Mac build number and the step that failed. Don’t include passwords, pairing codes or private desktop content.
