Before you buy

You need iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and macOS 13 or later. Install each app separately. Your Mac must be awake and unlocked, with Screen Recording and Accessibility allowed, on the same local network as your phone or tablet.

Home Mac shares your main display. Internet access, audio, file transfer and waking a sleeping Mac aren’t included. Fill screen crops the edges; Show whole desktop preserves the complete picture. VoiceOver cannot read controls within the remote desktop image.
