Home Mac is made by Krishneil Kristopher Singh, trading as Singh Studio, in New Zealand.

The app and companion

Home Mac does not operate a cloud relay, advertising system, analytics service or product account. We do not receive your desktop images, keyboard input or pairing keys through the app.

The Mac companion sends desktop images directly to your paired iPhone or iPad over your local network. Your phone sends mouse and keyboard actions back to the Mac. Companion connections use TLS encryption. Legacy VNC connections are not encrypted and should only be used on a trusted network.

Information stored on your devices

Saved Mac names, connection details and preferences are stored locally. Pairing credentials and remembered legacy passwords are stored in Apple Keychain. The companion stores paired-device information and credentials locally so it can recognise your devices.

Remove a saved Mac from the phone to remove its connection and associated credential. Remove a paired device in the Mac companion to revoke its access. Deleting an app alone may not delete all Keychain entries, so remove saved connections and revoke devices first.

Permissions

  • Local Network: find and connect to your Mac.
  • Camera: scan a pairing code. Camera images are not uploaded to Singh Studio.
  • Screen Recording on Mac: capture the desktop during sharing.
  • Accessibility on Mac: carry out mouse and keyboard actions.

You can change these permissions in your device’s Settings or System Settings. You can pause sharing or quit the companion at any time.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases and may provide crash diagnostics or TestFlight feedback according to your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policies. Home Mac does not embed a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.

This website and support

These Home Mac pages do not add analytics scripts or cookies. The website host, GitHub Pages, may process connection information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. If you email support, we receive the address and information you send and use it to answer your request. Please avoid sending pairing codes, passwords or private desktop content.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about support correspondence, email kris@singhstudio.co.nz. We may update this page when the product or its data practices change; the date above identifies this version.
