/ The brief

Are you tired of living the same day, every day? The Rhythms Podcast starts there, and asks the harder question underneath: what if those same days, deliberately curated, could be the best of your life? A show about habits and routines, chosen on purpose.

/ The approach

The format carries the idea: every episode opens with a ten-minute reflection before the conversation begins, and the show kept its own rhythm by publishing fortnightly across the run. It was recorded, edited and produced in-house, then published to Apple, Spotify and iHeart.

  • Format & show design
  • Recording, edit & production
  • Publishing & syndication
  • Co-hosting, 26 episodes
