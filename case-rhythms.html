At a glance

The idea

The show asks whether the same days, deliberately curated, could be the best of your life. It’s about habits and routines chosen on purpose.

What I did

I designed the format and co‑hosted every episode with Hannah Medland.

How it went

The show ran across 2023 and 2024, and every episode kept to the format.

The format

Every episode opens with a ten-minute reflection before the conversation begins.

The show came out fortnightly for the whole run, which gave it a rhythm of its own.

Making and publishing it

I recorded, edited and produced the whole run in-house, then sent it out to the podcast apps.

26 episodes, one each fortnight.
