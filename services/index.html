    Questions about platforms

    What does Squarespace cost?

    You pay Squarespace directly for the plan. The Basic plan is about NZ$228 a year (November 2026) and suits most small organisations.

    Do you only use Squarespace?

    No. Squarespace is what I recommend for most organisations because your team can run it, but I’m happy to work on the platform you already use or prefer, whether that’s improving it, refreshing it or rebuilding it. Other platforms are priced on a call.

    Questions about timing

    Can you do it faster?

    Yes. A rush build takes about half the time and costs 30% more. I take one rush build at a time, so ask early.

    Questions about extras

    Who writes the content?

    Usually me. Communications is what I do: you bring the facts and how you like to sound, I write it, and you have the final say.

    I’m not GST-registered, so the price you see is the price you pay.
