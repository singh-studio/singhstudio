Services /and prices

I work in communications in the charity sector, so every website starts with what you need to say. Then I design and build it, usually on Squarespace so your team can run it, or on the platform you already use. Film, photography and audio come from me too. The prices are all here, and every new site comes with three months of care.

/ Not sure where to start?

A website fix is NZ$750. I spend a week on the site you already have and sort out what’s hurting it most: speed, search, broken pieces and the words on the home page. If you go on to a new site within three months, the NZ$750 comes off its price.

    / Questions

    What does Squarespace cost?

    You pay Squarespace directly for the plan. The Basic plan is about NZ$228 a year (November 2026) and suits most small organisations.

    Is GST included?

    I’m not GST-registered, so the price you see is the price you pay.

    What if we don’t fit a tier?

    Tell me what you need and I’ll give you a fixed price in writing. The tiers are a starting point.

    Do you only use Squarespace?

    No. Squarespace is what I recommend for most organisations because your team can run it, but I’m happy to work on the platform you already use or prefer, whether that’s improving it, refreshing it or rebuilding it. Other platforms are priced on a call.

    Can we start small?

    Yes. A fix or a Template site is a good first step, and you can build from there.

    Can you do it faster?

    Yes. A rush build takes about half the time and costs 30% more. I take one rush build at a time, so ask early.

    Who writes the words?

    Usually me. Communications is the trade: you bring the facts and the voice, I bring the words, and you get final say.

    Do you work outside Wellington?

    Yes, anywhere in New Zealand and further. Most of the work happens on video calls, and I’m happy to meet in person around Wellington.

    Do you have a charity rate?

    My prices are set with charities and trusts in mind, so there isn’t a separate rate. Starting with a fix or a Template site keeps the first step small.
