/ Charities and trusts

Websites for /charities and trusts

I work in communications in the charity sector, so I know what a charity website has to do: explain the cause quickly, make giving and joining easy, and stay simple for a small team or volunteers to update. I design and build them, usually on Squarespace, for charities and trusts across New Zealand.

See prices and start your enquiry → Or book a call

/01 What a charity site needs

The message first

Before any design, I write the words with you: what the charity does, who it is for and what a visitor should do next. A Designed site includes this, so the design is built around a message that is already settled.

Giving that works

Peniel Trust’s site takes donations and sends receipts out automatically. For appeals, the Giving Kit plugin shows progress as a bar or a thermometer and offers gift levels.

Easy for volunteers to update

I build on Squarespace so your team can run the site themselves, and a Designed site comes with an editing guide and training. At Tino Taonga, every page, person, resource and event is a post the team edits in Squarespace.

Programmes, events and resources

Events Explorer gives your events a list and a month calendar, Resource Library makes your downloads and guides searchable, and Team Directory lists your staff and volunteers. A Designed site includes one of these plugins.

/ Questions

How much does a charity website cost?

A Template site is NZ$3,500, a Designed site is NZ$7,500 and a Bespoke site starts at NZ$15,000. If you would rather begin with the site you have, a website fix is NZ$750, and it comes off a new site if you go ahead within three months. You also pay Squarespace directly for the plan, which is about NZ$228 a year for Basic (November 2026).

Can our volunteers update it?

Yes. Squarespace is what I recommend for most organisations because your team can run it. A Template site includes an hour of training, a Designed site includes an editing guide and training, and a Bespoke site includes video walkthroughs for your team.

Can we take donations online?

Yes. Giving with receipts, as on the Peniel Trust site, is one of the custom features in a Bespoke site. A Designed site can use the Giving Kit plugin to show appeal progress and gift levels.

Do you have a charity rate?

My prices are set with charities and trusts in mind, so there isn’t a separate rate. Starting with a fix or a Template site keeps the first step small.

Do you work with charities outside Wellington?

Yes, with charities and trusts anywhere in New Zealand. Most of the work happens on video calls, and I’m happy to meet in person if you are around Wellington.
