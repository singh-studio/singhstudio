How CCA counts a working day

Under CCA, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the Monday (or Tuesday) a holiday moves to when it falls on a weekend: Waitangi Day and Anzac Day
  • any day from 24 December to 5 January

Questions

What if the contract sets its own date?

If the construction contract says when a payment schedule must be given, that date applies. The 20 working days only apply when the contract is silent (s 22).

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Construction Contracts Act 2002, s 5 (“working day”) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
