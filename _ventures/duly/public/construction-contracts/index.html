How the CCA counts a working day

“Working day” means a day of the week (irrespective of whether or not work is actually carried out on that day) other than— (a) a Saturday, a Sunday, Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the Sovereign's birthday, Te Rā Aro ki a Matariki/Matariki Observance Day, and Labour Day; and (ab) if Waitangi Day or Anzac Day falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the following Monday; and (b) the day observed in the appropriate area as the anniversary of the province of which the area forms a part; and (c) for the purposes of sections 18, 22, 24A, 33, 35 to 37A, 46, 47, 59, and 66, a day in the period commencing on 24 December in any year and ending with the close of 5 January in the following year; and (d) for the purposes of sections 71B, 74, and 75, a day in the period commencing on 25 December in any year and ending with the close of 15 January in the following year.Construction Contracts Act 2002, s 5

Under the CCA, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the weekday Waitangi Day and Anzac Day are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 24 December to 5 January
  • the anniversary day of the province where the construction site is

Worked example

Under the CCA (Construction Contracts Act 2002, s 5), 20 working days after Tuesday 1 December 2026 is Monday 11 January 2027. Skipped besides weekends: Thu 24 Dec 2026 – Tue 5 Jan 2027 (24 December – 5 January period).

This 24 December to 5 January period applies to ss 18, 22, 24A, 33, 35–37A, 46, 47, 59 and 66. Sections 71B, 74 and 75 use 25 December to 15 January instead, and the retention-money sections (18C, 18K) aren’t in either list.

The Act skips the anniversary day “observed in the appropriate area”, which it doesn’t define (MBIE’s prescribed forms leave it out). Duly uses the province where the site is.

Questions

What if the contract sets its own date?

If the construction contract says when a payment schedule must be given, that applies. The 20 working days only apply when the contract is silent (s 22(b)).

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Which anniversary day applies?

The CCA skips the anniversary day of the province where the construction site is. Anniversary days follow the old provincial boundaries and local custom, so check with the local council if you are near a boundary.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Construction Contracts Act 2002, s 5 and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
