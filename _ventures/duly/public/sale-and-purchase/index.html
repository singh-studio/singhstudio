How ADLS agreement counts a working day

Under ADLS agreement, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the Monday (or Tuesday) a holiday moves to when it falls on a weekend: Waitangi Day and Anzac Day
  • any day from 24 December to 5 January
  • the anniversary day of the province where the property is

Worked example

Under ADLS agreement (ADLS/REINZ Agreement for Sale and Purchase of Real Estate, cl 1 (“working day”)), 10 working days after Tuesday 1 December 2026 is Tuesday 15 December 2026.

Questions

What time does a working day end?

The standard agreement treats a working day as running from 9am to 5pm. A notice served after 5pm, or on a day that isn’t a working day, is treated as served on the next working day.

Which anniversary day applies?

The anniversary day of the province where the property is, so an Auckland property skips Auckland Anniversary Day and a Wellington property skips Wellington Anniversary Day.

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Which anniversary day applies?

ADLS agreement skips the anniversary day of the province where the property is. Anniversary days follow the old provincial boundaries and local custom, so check with the local council if you are near a boundary.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies ADLS/REINZ Agreement for Sale and Purchase of Real Estate, cl 1 (“working day”) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
