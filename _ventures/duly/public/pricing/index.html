Questions

Do I need to pay to use the calculators?

No. The website, the shareable links, the calendar feeds and the embeddable calculator are free. Paid plans are for software, AI assistants and teams that call the API or MCP server at volume or commercially.

How do I get my key?

Straight after checkout you land on a page showing your API key. There’s no account to create. Keep the key somewhere safe; you can manage or cancel the subscription any time from the billing page.

Are prices in New Zealand dollars?

Yes, NZD. Invoices come from Stripe, and you can add your business details at checkout.

What happens if a rule changes?

When Parliament changes a definition or a new holiday is set, Duly is updated and the change is listed in the changelog. Every answer carries the date the rules were last checked.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from the billing portal. Your key keeps working to the end of the period you have paid for.
