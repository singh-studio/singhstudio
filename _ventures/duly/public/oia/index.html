How the OIA counts a working day

“Working day” means any day of the week other than— (a) a Saturday, a Sunday, Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the Sovereign's birthday, Te Rā Aro ki a Matariki/Matariki Observance Day, and Labour Day; and (ab) if Waitangi Day or Anzac Day falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the following Monday; and (b) a day in the period commencing with 25 December in any year and ending with 15 January in the following year.Official Information Act 1982, s 2(1)

Under the OIA, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the weekday Waitangi Day and Anzac Day are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 25 December to 15 January

Worked example

Under the OIA (Official Information Act 1982, s 2(1)), 20 working days after Tuesday 1 December 2026 is Wednesday 20 January 2027. Skipped besides weekends: Fri 25 Dec 2026 – Fri 15 Jan 2027 (25 December – 15 January period).

Questions

How long does an agency have to answer an OIA request?

It must decide as soon as reasonably practicable, and no later than 20 working days after the day it receives the request (s 15(1)). It can extend that limit by notice under s 15A, given within the original 20 working days.

Does the Christmas break count?

No. No day from 25 December to 15 January is a working day under the OIA, so a request made in mid-December usually isn’t due until February.

Do anniversary days count?

Yes. The OIA’s definition doesn’t mention provincial anniversary days, so they count as working days even if the agency’s office is closed. (Councils are different: see the LGOIMA calculator.)

What if the agency asks me to clarify my request?

If you amend or clarify a request, the agency may treat it as a new request that replaces the original (s 15(1AA)), so the 20 working days can start again. Where the agency asked for the clarification, it can only do that if it asked within 7 working days of receiving the request (s 15(1AB)).

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Official Information Act 1982, s 2(1) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
