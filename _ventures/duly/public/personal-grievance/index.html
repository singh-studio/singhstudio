How the Employment Relations Act counts a working day

Under the Employment Relations Act, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the weekday Waitangi Day, Anzac Day, New Year's Day and 2 January are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 25 December to 2 January

Questions

Does the first day count?

Yes. The 90 days begin with the day of the action (or the day it came to your notice, if later), so that day is day 1 (Legislation Act 2019, s 54).

What if day 90 is a weekend or holiday?

Then raising it on the next working day is in time (Legislation Act 2019, s 55). Duly moves the date for you.

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Employment Relations Act 2000 (no own definition; Legislation Act 2019, s 13 applies) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
