How the RTA counts a working day

“Working day” means any day other than— (a) a Saturday, a Sunday, Waitangi Day, Anzac Day, the Sovereign's birthday, Te Rā Aro ki a Matariki/Matariki Observance Day, and Labour Day; and (b) if Waitangi Day or Anzac Day falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the following Monday; and (c) the day observed in the appropriate area as the anniversary of the province of which the area forms part; and (d) a day in the period commencing with Good Friday and expiring with the Tuesday after Easter; and (e) a day in the period commencing with 24 December and expiring with 3 January.Residential Tenancies Act 1986, s 2(1)

Under the RTA, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • any day from Good Friday to Easter Tuesday
  • the weekday Waitangi Day and Anzac Day are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 24 December to 3 January
  • the anniversary day of the province where the premises is

When a notice counts as given

  • Handed to the person (s 136(1)(a))
  • Emailed before 5pm (s 136(8))
  • Emailed after 5pm (next working day) (s 136(8))
  • Put in the mailbox or on the door (2nd working day) (s 136(7))
  • Posted (4th working day) (s 136(6))

Notice periods start the day after the notice is given or treated as given, and end at the close of the last day (s 136A). Choose how the notice was given and Duly does both steps.

The RTA closes 24 December to 3 January only, and skips Good Friday to Easter Tuesday. Read literally, it doesn’t exclude a New Year holiday observed on 4 January (as in 2027 and 2028), so Duly counts it and says so.

Questions

When does a posted notice count as given?

On the fourth working day after posting, unless there is evidence otherwise (s 136(6)). A notice put in the mailbox or attached to the door counts as given on the second working day after delivery (s 136(7)), and an email sent after 5pm counts as given the next working day (s 136(8)).

How much notice does a landlord have to give?

For a periodic tenancy, at least 90 days in any case, or at least 42 days if the owner or a family member is moving in, or the property has been sold with vacant possession (s 51). A tenant must give at least 21 days (s 51(2A)).

Which working days does the RTA skip?

Weekends, Waitangi Day, Anzac Day (and their Mondayised dates), the King’s Birthday, Matariki, Labour Day, Good Friday to Easter Tuesday, 24 December to 3 January, and the anniversary day where the premises are.

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Which anniversary day applies?

The RTA skips the anniversary day of the province where the premises is. Anniversary days follow the old provincial boundaries and local custom, so check with the local council if you are near a boundary.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Residential Tenancies Act 1986, s 2(1) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
