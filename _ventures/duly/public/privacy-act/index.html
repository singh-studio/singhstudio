How the Privacy Act counts a working day

“Working day” means any day of the week other than— (a) a Saturday, a Sunday, Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the Sovereign's birthday, Te Rā Aro ki a Matariki/Matariki Observance Day, or Labour Day; or (b) if Waitangi Day or Anzac Day falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the following Monday; or (c) a day in the period commencing on 25 December in one year and ending with 15 January in the next year.Privacy Act 2020, s 7(1)

Under the Privacy Act, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the weekday Waitangi Day and Anzac Day are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 25 December to 15 January

Worked example

Under the Privacy Act (Privacy Act 2020, s 7(1)), 20 working days after Tuesday 1 December 2026 is Wednesday 20 January 2027. Skipped besides weekends: Fri 25 Dec 2026 – Fri 15 Jan 2027 (25 December – 15 January period).

Questions

How long does an agency have to respond?

As soon as reasonably practicable, and no later than 20 working days after the day it receives the request (s 44(1)), unless it extends the limit under s 48.

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Privacy Act 2020, s 7(1) and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
