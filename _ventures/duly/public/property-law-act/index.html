How the Property Law Act counts a working day

“Working day” means a day of the week other than— (a) a Saturday, a Sunday, Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the Sovereign's birthday, Te Rā Aro ki a Matariki/Matariki Observance Day, and Labour Day; and (ab) if Waitangi Day or Anzac Day falls on a Saturday or a Sunday, the following Monday; and (b) a day in the period commencing with 25 December and ending with 2 January in the following year; and (c) the day observed as the anniversary of any province in which an act is to be done.Property Law Act 2007, s 4

Under the Property Law Act, a day is not a working day if it is:

  • a Saturday or Sunday
  • Waitangi Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Anzac Day, the King's Birthday, Matariki and Labour Day
  • the weekday Waitangi Day and Anzac Day are observed on when they fall on a weekend
  • any day from 25 December to 2 January
  • the anniversary day of the province where the place where the act is to be done is

Worked example

Under the Property Law Act (Property Law Act 2007, s 4), 20 working days after Tuesday 1 December 2026 is Wednesday 6 January 2027. Skipped besides weekends: Fri 25 Dec 2026 – Sat 2 Jan 2027 (25 December – 2 January period).

Questions

Does the day I received it count?

Not normally. Where a period runs “after” a day, that day is left out, so day 1 is the next working day. Duly counts that way by default. If your contract counts “from and including” a day, open More options and tick “Count the start date itself as day 1”.

Which anniversary day applies?

The Property Law Act skips the anniversary day of the province where the place where the act is to be done is. Anniversary days follow the old provincial boundaries and local custom, so check with the local council if you are near a boundary.

Can I rely on this?

Duly applies Property Law Act 2007, s 4 and shows every day it skipped, so you can check the answer by hand. It isn’t legal advice: the timeframe provision itself, any extension, a contract term or a court direction can change the answer.
