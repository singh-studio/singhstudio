Privacy

Last updated Wednesday 21 October 2026

The calculators run in your browser. The dates you enter aren’t sent to us and nothing on the website tracks you.

When you call the API or MCP server, our host (Cloudflare) processes the request, including your IP address, to answer it and enforce rate limits. We record aggregate usage (plan, endpoint and regime) to run the service, without storing the dates you send.

If you subscribe, Stripe handles your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. We keep your API key (stored only as a one-way hash), the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, and the email address you paid with, so we can run your plan and help if you lose your key.

We don’t sell personal information. You can ask to see or correct what we hold about you under the Privacy Act 2020 by emailing kris@singhstudio.co.nz.
