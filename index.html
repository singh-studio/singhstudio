06 / Kris

Run by Kris Singh.
Built around your message.

I’ve worked in communications since 2014, almost all of it for organisations doing good work. The core of it is strategy and writing: positioning and messaging, brand voice, editorial, press and media, fundraising appeals, and the policy that keeps a whole team saying the same thing.

Singh Studio is where I do the hands-on work. I started it in 2020 to build websites for teams I already knew, including The Salvation Army’s Next Gen and Creative Ministries teams, and it now covers apps and AI workflows as well, with photography, film and sound when a job needs them. Having spent so long on the client side, I know what an appeal, a board paper or a press release has to achieve before anyone looks at the design.

You’ll deal with me from the first meeting through to launch and the fixes afterwards. I write the words, design and build the thing, and I’m the person who answers when something needs to change.
