/ Questions

Do you meet in person?

Yes, around Wellington, over coffee or at your office. When it is simpler for you, we use video calls instead.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size of the site. Template is NZ$3,500 for up to 6 pages, Designed is NZ$7,500 for up to 12 pages, and Bespoke is from NZ$15,000, with most landing between NZ$15,000 and NZ$30,000. I’m not GST-registered, so the price you see is the price you pay.

Can you improve the site we already have?

Yes. A Refresh starts at NZ$2,500 and improves the site on the platform you already use. For a smaller first step, a website fix is NZ$750: a week on your existing site, covering speed, search, broken pieces and the words on the home page.

Who hosts the site?

Squarespace hosts Squarespace sites, and you pay Squarespace directly for the plan. If you are on another platform, we agree how it is hosted on a call.

How long does a website take?

A Template site takes about 2 to 3 weeks, a Designed site about 5 to 7 weeks and a Bespoke site about 8 to 14 weeks. A rush build takes about half the time for 30% more, and I take one rush build at a time, so ask early.
