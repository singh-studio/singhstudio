Bearers

Three records of worship written with young Salvationists and released between 2016 and 2018.

Book a call
Client
The Salvation Army, NZ, Fiji & Tonga
Years
2016 to 2018
My part
  • Project concept & A&R
  • Co-writing with six young writers
  • Production, recording & release
  • Live launch at a national congress

At a glance

What they needed

A place for worship written by the movement’s own young people, in their own sound.

What I did

I came up with the project, then co‑wrote, recorded and produced the music.

What changed

Young Salvationists wrote and fronted the record themselves, and it was launched live at the Now is the Time congress in Wellington.

Songs looking for somewhere to go

At the Army’s Amplify creative arts camps, the same thing kept coming up: young songwriters with real songs and nowhere to take them.

I ran Bearers from inside The Salvation Army’s Creative Ministries Department.

6 young Salvationist writers got in a room together, and the songs kept coming back to identity.
