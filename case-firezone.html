Firezone

Client
The Salvation Army — Youth
Years
2023
Scope
Web · Brand
Role
Design & build

/ The brief

One digital home base was missing: events, resources and identity for a youth network spanning Aotearoa, Fiji, Tonga and Samoa, a community spread across the Pacific.

/ The approach

A platform build with a content model shaped around regions and events, designed to be maintained by a small national team and read on the phones it would actually be opened on.

  • Platform design & build
  • Brand system
  • Content architecture
  • Team handover

/ The outcome

Reach
Youth across NZ, Fiji, Tonga & Samoa
Built for
Small-team maintenance, mobile-first reading
Shipped
Brand and build together, 2023

“Four countries, one platform, and a small team who can run it themselves.”
