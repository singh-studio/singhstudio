/ The brief

Tino taonga, an invaluable treasure: that is how The Salvation Army sees the children and young people in its care. The leaders doing that work were spread across a whole territory with no one place to find help. They needed a hub.

/ The approach

The site is built for one moment: a leader, mid-week, hunting for the right resource. A custom menu keeps Find a Resource one click from everywhere. Behind it sits a dated, searchable library of Bible studies, training, funding guidance and pastoral-care material, and the ministry team publishes to it without touching a developer.

  • Custom menu & information architecture
  • Resource centre design
  • Team-run publishing
  • News & team pages

/ The outcome

One hub
“Your hub for Next Generation Ministry”, live across the territory
Findable
A resource centre with search, dates and steady updates
Self-run
The ministry team publishes news and resources themselves

“Find a Resource sits one click from every page. That was the whole argument.”
